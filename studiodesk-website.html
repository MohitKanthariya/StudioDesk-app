Everything you need

Built for photographers.
Designed for growth.

Every feature in StudioDesk is crafted to save you time and make your studio look more professional.

01
Smart Quotations
Create detailed photography and industrial quotations with line items, GST toggle, and instant PDF generation. Send to clients in one tap.
02
Client CRM
Keep a full database of clients with contact info, source tracking, quote history, and one-tap calling. Never lose a lead again.
03
PDF Templates
Choose from Luxury Gold, Clean Minimal, or Bold Industrial templates. Each one makes your quotes look like they're from a high-end studio.
04
Live Dashboard
See your monthly revenue, pending payments, active clients, and quote status — all at a glance from your home screen.
05
Deal Confirmation
Track quotes from draft to sent to confirmed. Use the "Confirm Deal" flow to lock in bookings and move them out of pending.
06
GST Included
Toggle GST on or off per quotation. Total amounts are calculated automatically. Stay compliant without the hassle.
